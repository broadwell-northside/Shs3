A family business, built on referrals

SHS & Improvements is Erick Von Borries and his wife, a husband-and-wife team who have been remodeling homes for more than ten years. We're a small company on purpose. It means one of us is involved in every job, and the people you meet at the estimate are the people who answer for the finished work.

We treat your home like it's our own: protecting your landscaping, cleaning up at the end of every day, and paying attention to the details most people never see, like flashing behind siding, solid footings under a deck and a clean caulk line.

We've never needed much advertising. Most of our customers find us because a neighbor recommended us, and we work hard to keep it that way.
